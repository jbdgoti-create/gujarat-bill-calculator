How this bill is calculated

Energy charge: telescopic slabs. Each rate applies only to the units inside that slab. Energy is billed on complete units (partial units are dropped).

Fixed charge: by connected load (state companies) or supply phase (Torrent). It is also the minimum bill.

FPPAS: units x the fuel surcharge rate. It is revised every quarter, so copy the rate printed on your latest bill. State companies: Rs 2.45 plus 3.99% variable surcharge from July 2026, calculated on the bill before duty.

Electricity duty: 15% urban and 7.5% rural, on energy + fixed + FPPAS (Gujarat Electricity Duty Act, 1958).

Sources: GERC tariff schedules effective 01-04-2026 for DGVCL, MGVCL, PGVCL, UGVCL, Torrent Power Ahmedabad and Torrent Power Surat. DGVCL, MGVCL, PGVCL and UGVCL share one tariff. Torrent supplies urban areas only.

This is an estimate, not an official bill. Meter charges, arrears, late fees, subsidies and BPL concessions are not included. Always compare with your actual bill.
